<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="States">

On and off come from `checked` or `defaultChecked`. The thumb slides across and the track changes
color. Hover and press darken the track and stretch the thumb a little; a disabled or loading
switch shows neither. Disabled grays out the track and the label.

<Example of={SwitchStories.States} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Loading">

Set `isLoading` while a change is being saved. A Spinner replaces the mark, and the switch ignores
clicks and Space, so it can't be flipped again until the save finishes. It isn't disabled: it stays
in the tab order and keeps focus, with `aria-busy` set. The label doesn't change. Clear `isLoading`
and set `checked` to the saved value when the save finishes.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Marks">

The check and X marks show by default. Set `showIcons={false}` for a plain track where the track
color reads clearly on its own. The loading Spinner shows either way.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Controlled or uncontrolled">

Pass `checked` with `onCheckedChange` to keep the value in your state, or `defaultChecked` to let
the input keep it. `onCheckedChange` gets the next value as a boolean, then the change event.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Labels and motion">

The switch sits before the label and is centered on it, so when a label wraps the switch lines up
with the middle of the text, not the first line. The thumb's slide and the track's color change
are switched off when someone asks for reduced motion.

<Example of={SwitchStories.EdgeCases} />

</Subsection>

<Section title="Do and don't">

<Guidelines>
<Do caption="Name the setting in the label. The switch shows whether it's on." code={`<Switch label="Email notifications" />`}>
  <Switch label="Email notifications" />
</Do>
<Dont caption="Write the label as an instruction. It reads wrong once the switch is on." code={`<Switch label="Turn on email notifications" />`}>
  <Switch label="Turn on email notifications" defaultChecked />
</Dont>
<Do caption="Set isLoading while the change saves. The switch stays focused and can't be flipped twice." code={`<Switch label="Sync calendar" checked={on} isLoading={saving} onCheckedChange={save} />`}>
  <Switch label="Sync calendar" defaultChecked isLoading />
</Do>
<Dont caption="Disable it while the change saves. It drops out of the tab order, so keyboard focus is lost, and it reads as unavailable." code={`<Switch label="Sync calendar" checked={on} disabled={saving} onCheckedChange={save} />`}>
  <Switch label="Sync calendar" defaultChecked disabled />
</Dont>
<Do caption="Use Switch for a setting that applies as soon as it's flipped." code={`<Switch label="Auto-archive closed matters" checked={autoArchive} onCheckedChange={saveAutoArchive} />`}>
  <Switch label="Auto-archive closed matters" defaultChecked />

<Section title="Accessibility">

- Switch renders a native `input type="checkbox"` with `role="switch"` inside a `label`, so it's
  focusable, announced as a switch that's on or off, toggled by Space, and named by the label text.
  Don't rebuild it with `role="switch"` on another element.
- Without a visible label, name it with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. Nothing warns you when
  both are missing.
- `isLoading` sets `aria-busy` and ignores clicks and Space, but doesn't disable the input, so it
  stays in the tab order. The Spinner is hidden from assistive technology, so `aria-busy` carries the
  state.
- The track, thumb, and marks are hidden from assistive technology.
- `disabled` and `required` are the native attributes: a disabled switch leaves the tab order, and a
  required one is announced as required. The `*` is hidden from assistive technology, so it isn't
  read as part of the name.
- Keyboard focus shows the shared Focus Ring around the track.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves focus to the switch, loading or not. A disabled switch is skipped.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">Turns the switch on or off, unless it's loading.</Key>
</Keyboard>
