<Section title="When to use">

<Usage>
<UseFor>

- The people on a record, where a row of photos is enough and names can wait: a matter's team, a
  task's assignees, a document's collaborators.
- A list of people that can grow, with `maxVisible` keeping the row to a fixed length.
- A mix of people and firms, with `entityType="team"` on each firm.

</UseFor>
<UseInstead>

- **One person:** <DocLink to="Components/Data Display/Avatar" />.
- **People whose names need to be read at a glance:** a list of names, or <DocLink to="Components/Data Display/Tag Group" /> with a Tag for each person.
- **Choosing people:** a picker, such as <DocLink to="Components/Forms/Select" />. Avatar Group only shows them.

</UseInstead>
</Usage>

</Section>

<Section title="Anatomy">

<Anatomy specimen={<AvatarGroup avatars={people(6)} maxVisible={3} />}>
  <Part name="root" target={avatarGroupStyles.root}>A `div` that lays the avatars out in a row, each one overlapping the one before it.</Part>
  <Part name="visible avatars" target={avatarGroupStyles.avatarRing}>The first `maxVisible` people, each a real Avatar in a 1px ring that keeps the circles apart. All of them show when `maxVisible` is unset.</Part>
  <Part name="overflow trigger" target={avatarGroupStyles.overflowTrigger}>Only when people are hidden: a Button reshaped into a circle the size of an avatar, reading `+3` by default. It opens the overflow panel.</Part>
  <Part name="overflow panel">Shown only while open: a Dropdown Menu listing each hidden person by name, with a small avatar beside the name.</Part>

<Section title="Variants">

<Subsection title="Sizes">

Four sizes, from `xs` (24px) to `lg` (48px), with `lg` the default. One `size` sets every avatar and
the overflow button, so the circles always match; there's no size per person. Avatars overlap by 4px
at `xs` and `sm`, 8px at `md`, and 12px at `lg`. Avatar's `xxs` and `xl` sizes aren't part of Avatar
Group.

<Example of={AvatarGroupStories.Stack} caption="Every size, for people and for firms with entityType team." />

</Subsection>

</Section>

<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="Overflow">

With no `maxVisible`, every avatar shows and there's no overflow button. Once there are more people
than `maxVisible`, the first `maxVisible` show and the rest move into the overflow menu, in order. At
least one avatar always shows beside the button: a `maxVisible` below 1 is treated as 1, with a
warning in development.

<Example of={AvatarGroupStories.Overflow} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Choosing someone hidden">

The overflow button opens and closes the menu. Each row shows the person's name, or `alt` when there's
no name, and their photo or fallback as a small decorative avatar; a person with `isDisabled` is a
disabled row. Their other Avatar props, such as `onClick`, `presence`, and `status`, don't carry into
the menu.

Choosing a row calls `onOverflowAvatarSelect` with that person and the event. The menu then closes
and focus returns to the overflow button. Avatar Group doesn't navigate: open the person's profile
from the callback. To keep the menu open, call `event.preventDefault()` in it.

<Example of={AvatarGroupStories.EdgeCases} caption="Choosing a hidden person, one person under maxVisible, interactive avatars, and maxVisible below 1." />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Each avatar is an Avatar">

Every prop on an item except `size` goes to its Avatar, so a visible avatar can have a photo, a
presence badge, or its own `onClick`. Set `entityType="team"` on a firm or another organization: its
fallback shows two people instead of one, in the row and in the menu. Each avatar paints over the one
before it, and the overflow button paints over the last.

<Example of={AvatarGroupStories.Content} caption="A task's assignees, a matter team past maxVisible, and clients with an outside firm." />

</Subsection>

</Section>

<Section title="Do and don't">

<Guidelines>
<Do caption="Set maxVisible on a list that can grow, so the row stays the same length." code={`<AvatarGroup avatars={team} maxVisible={4} size="md" />`}>
  <AvatarGroup avatars={people(9)} maxVisible={4} size="md" />
</Do>
<Dont caption="Show every person on a long list. The row keeps growing and crowds what's beside it." code={`<AvatarGroup avatars={team} size="md" />`}>
  <AvatarGroup avatars={people(9)} size="md" />
</Dont>
<Do
  caption="Open the person from onOverflowAvatarSelect, which is the only thing a menu row calls."
  code={`<AvatarGroup
    avatars={team}
    maxVisible={4}
    onOverflowAvatarSelect={(person) => openProfile(person.id)}
/>`}
/>
<Dont
  caption="Rely on a person's own onClick once they're hidden. The menu row doesn't call it."
  code={`<AvatarGroup
    avatars={team.map((person) => ({ ...person, onClick: () => openProfile(person.id) }))}
    maxVisible={4}
/>`}
/>
<Do caption="Give every person a name. It names their avatar and their row in the menu." code={`{ id: 'person-1', name: 'Ben Averill', src: photo }`} />
<Dont caption="Leave out the name and the alt. The avatar has no accessible name, and its menu row reads “Unnamed”." code={`{ id: 'person-1', src: photo }`} />
</Guidelines>

</Section>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- The root is a `div` with no role or name, and Avatar Group adds nothing to the visible avatars:
  each is an Avatar, an image named by its `name`, or a button when it's interactive. Say what the
  people are in the text around the group, such as a “Team” heading.
- The overflow button is a native `button`. Its accessible name says what it reveals:
  `getOverflowButtonLabel`, “+3 more people” by default (“+1 more person” for one). It starts with
  the visible text, `+3`, so people using speech input can say what they see; if you change
  `overflowLabel`, change `getOverflowButtonLabel` to match, and translate it with the rest of your
  interface. It gets `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` from Dropdown Menu.
- The menu has `role="menu"` and is named by `overflowMenuAriaLabel`, “3 more people” by default.
  Each row is a `menuitem` named by the person's name; its avatar is decorative. Translate
  `overflowMenuAriaLabel` with the rest of your interface. A person with no name or `alt` gets the
  row name `unnamedLabel`, “Unnamed” by default.
- Opening the menu moves focus to the first enabled row. Choosing a row, Escape, or Tab out of the
  menu closes it and returns focus to the overflow button.
- Focus shows the shared Focus Ring on the overflow button and on interactive avatars.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves through the interactive avatars, then to the overflow button. Static avatars aren't tab stops. In the open menu, closes it and returns focus to the overflow button.</Key>
  <Key keys="Enter">On the overflow button, opens or closes the menu. On a row, chooses that person, closes the menu, and returns focus to the button.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">The same as Enter.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Down">In the menu, moves to the next enabled row, wrapping from the last to the first.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Up">In the menu, moves to the previous enabled row, wrapping from the first to the last.</Key>
  <Key keys="Home">In the menu, moves to the first enabled row.</Key>
  <Key keys="End">In the menu, moves to the last enabled row.</Key>
  <Key keys="Escape">Closes the menu and returns focus to the overflow button.</Key>
</Keyboard>

</Section>
