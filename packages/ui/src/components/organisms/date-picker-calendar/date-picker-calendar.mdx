<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="Picking a day">

A click on a day picks it and calls `onChange`. A day from the month before or after can be picked
too, and the calendar moves to its month. Without `value`, the calendar keeps the pick itself,
starting from `defaultValue`; with `value`, it shows only what you pass.

<Example of={DatePickerCalendarStories.Controlled} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Moving between months">

The header buttons move back or forward one month or one year, and the title changes with them.
The calendar opens on `defaultMonth`, else the chosen date's month, else today's. Pass `month` and
`onMonthChange` to control the month on view.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Restricting dates">

`min` and `max` set the earliest and latest dates that can be picked, and `isDateDisabled` rules out
single dates, such as weekends. Ruled-out days are muted and can't be picked. A month or year button
is disabled when every day of the month it would show is out of range.

<Example of={DatePickerCalendarStories.MinMax} caption="Only 4 to 24 March can be picked, so the month and year buttons are disabled." />

<Example of={DatePickerCalendarStories.DisabledWeekends} caption="isDateDisabled rules out weekends." />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Week start and language">

`weekStartsOn` sets the first column, from `0` (Sunday, the default) to `6` (Saturday). `locale`
sets the language of the month, the weekday names, and each day's accessible name. It doesn't change
the week start, so set both for a region whose week starts on Monday.

<Example of={DatePickerCalendarStories.WeekStartsMonday} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Day states">

Days of the month on view use the subtle text color, and turn the default color on a neutral fill
when hovered. Today uses the selected text color with a short line under the number. The chosen day
has the selected fill, border, and text. Days outside the month, and days that can't be picked, use
the disabled text color. The calendar itself has no fill: the stories here wrap it in Popup's
surface, as Date Picker does.

<Example of={DatePickerCalendarStories.Playground} />

</Subsection>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- The days are a `table` with `role="grid"`, named by the month title. The title is a polite live
  region, so a change of month is announced.
- Each weekday heading shows a short name and is named with the full one, such as "Monday".
- Each day is a native `button` in a `gridcell`, named with its full date, such as "Monday, March 9,
  2026". Today has `aria-current="date"`, and the chosen day's cell has `aria-selected="true"`.
- A day that can't be picked has `aria-disabled="true"` and stays focusable, so people can move
  across it. The keys aren't limited by `min` and `max` either: focus can move to a day outside them,
  but that day can't be picked.
- Only one day is in the tab order. It starts on the chosen day, or today, when that's in the month
  on view, else on the first of the month. The keys below move focus from there, and focus shows the
  shared Focus Ring.
- The header buttons are Icon Buttons named "Previous year", "Previous month", "Next month", and
  "Next year". Change the names with `previousYearLabel`, `previousMonthLabel`, `nextMonthLabel`,
  and `nextYearLabel`, for example to translate them. A disabled button leaves the tab order.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves through the enabled month and year buttons, then to the one focusable day.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Left">Moves to the previous day.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Right">Moves to the next day.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Up">Moves to the same day last week.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Down">Moves to the same day next week.</Key>
  <Key keys="Home">Moves to the first day of the week, by `weekStartsOn`.</Key>
  <Key keys="End">Moves to the last day of the week.</Key>
  <Key keys="Page Up">Moves to the same day last month, or the month's last day when it's shorter.</Key>
  <Key keys="Page Down">Moves to the same day next month, or the month's last day when it's shorter.</Key>
  <Key keys="Shift + Page Up">Moves to the same day last year.</Key>
  <Key keys="Shift + Page Down">Moves to the same day next year.</Key>
  <Key keys="Enter">Picks the focused day, unless it can't be picked.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">Picks the focused day, unless it can't be picked.</Key>
</Keyboard>

When a key moves focus to a day in another month, the calendar shows that month.

</Section>
