<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="One value">

Both halves show one `Date`. Picking a date keeps the time already chosen, or midnight when there's
none. Confirming a time keeps the date already chosen, or `today` when there's none, which defaults
to the current date. Each change calls `onChange` with the whole date and time.

<Example of={DateTimePickerStories.Controlled} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Each half">

The date half works like Date Picker: picking a day closes its calendar. The time half works like
Time Picker: choices are held until they're confirmed. Each half opens its own popup, and there's no
prop to open one from outside. `min`, `max`, `isDateDisabled`, and `weekStartsOn` go to the calendar;
`minuteStep` goes to the time columns; `locale` goes to both. `datePlaceholder`, `timePlaceholder`,
`dateFormat`, and `timeFormat` set each half's empty text and format.

Each half's built-in text passes through, for a translation. `dateDialogLabel` and
`timeDialogLabel` name the calendar and the time panel (each half's `dialogLabel`). The calendar's
`previousMonthLabel`, `nextMonthLabel`, `previousYearLabel`, and `nextYearLabel` keep its names. The time half's
`confirmLabel`, `cancelLabel`, `hoursLabel`, `minutesLabel`, `meridiemLabel`, `amLabel`, and
`pmLabel` keep Time Picker's names. Any left out keep the half's English default. Month and weekday
names follow `locale`.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="States">

`disabled` disables both halves. `invalid` gives both the error border and sets `aria-invalid` on
both inputs; in a Field with `state="error"`, Field sets it for you.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Width">

The control is 480px wide: two 240px halves, side by side on one line.

</Subsection>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- The control is a `div` with `role="group"`. Name it with Field, whose label names the group, or
  with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`. `aria-label` is ignored when `aria-labelledby` is set.
- Field's label points at the date input, so clicking the label focuses the date half and opens
  its calendar. The label still names the group, and the date input keeps its own name, "Date".
- Each half is a read-only `input` named by `dateLabel` or `timeLabel`, "Date" and "Time" by default,
  so it's announced with the group's name around it.
- In a Field, the message describes the group. An error sets `aria-invalid` on both inputs, and
  `required` sets both inputs' native `required`. The form then checks both halves: submitting
  without a date or a time stops the submit, and the browser points at the empty half.
- Each half's popup, focus, and announcements are its own: see <DocLink to="Components/Forms/Date Picker" />
  and <DocLink to="Components/Forms/Time Picker" />.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves focus to the date half, then to the time half. A disabled control is skipped.</Key>
  <Key keys="Enter">Opens the focused half's calendar or time columns.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">Opens the focused half's calendar or time columns.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Down">Opens the focused half's calendar or time columns.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Up">Opens the focused half's calendar or time columns.</Key>
  <Key keys="Escape">Closes the open half and returns focus to its field. The time half throws away its choices.</Key>
</Keyboard>

The keys inside each popup are on the Date Picker, Date Picker Calendar, and Time Picker pages.
