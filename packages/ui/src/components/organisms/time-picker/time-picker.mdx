<Section title="Anatomy">

<Anatomy specimen={<TimePicker defaultValue={NINE_THIRTY} aria-label="Hearing time" />}>
  <Part name="root" target={timePickerStyles.root}>A `div` that holds the field and takes any other attribute you pass. 240px wide by default, and never wider than its container.</Part>
  <Part name="trigger" target={timePickerStyles.trigger}>A read-only Text Field showing the formatted time, with a clock icon after it. Clicking it opens the panel, and clicking it again confirms.</Part>

<Section title="Variants">

<Subsection title="Sizes">

Three heights: `sm` (32px), `md` (40px), and `lg` (48px), the same as Text Field, so a Time Picker
lines up with the fields beside it. The panel is the same size for every field size.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.Sizes} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Context">

`context` is Text Field's. `default` is the bordered field for forms. `inline` is for a field inside a
row of other content: no border or background until someone hovers it. Field passes
`context="inline"` when it's inline.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.Contexts} />

</Subsection>

</Section>

<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="Opening">

A click on the field opens the panel below it. Enter, Space, Arrow Down, and Arrow Up open it from the
keyboard. Opening moves focus to the chosen hour, and while the panel is open the field keeps its
focus style. With no value, the columns start at 12:00 AM.

The field is read-only: people pick from the columns and can't type a time.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.Playground} caption="Click the field, choose an hour and minutes, then Confirm." />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Confirming and cancelling">

Choices in the columns are held until they're confirmed, and the field shows them as they change.
Confirm, a click outside the panel, or a second click on the field saves them: `onChange` is called
and the panel closes. A click outside saves even when nothing changed. Cancel or Escape throws the
choices away, and the field goes back to the saved time. Confirm, Cancel, Escape, and a second click
return focus to the field; a click outside leaves it where the click put it.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Minute step">

`minuteStep` sets the minutes between the options in the minutes column, counting from 00, and
defaults to 5. A saved time whose minutes fall between steps opens on the nearest step, and saving
keeps that step. When the minutes round up to the next hour, such as 10:58, it opens on 10:00 instead
of 11:00; that's a known bug.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.MinuteStep} caption="A 15-minute step: 00, 15, 30, and 45." />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The value is a date">

Time Picker's value is a `Date`, and only its hours and minutes are read. The `Date` that `onChange`
passes keeps the saved value's day, or today's when there's no value yet. Read only the hours and
minutes from it, or use Date Time Picker when the day matters.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Format and labels">

The field shows the time in the locale's 12-hour format, such as 04:30 PM in US English. Pass `format`
to show it another way. The columns are always 12-hour. `confirmLabel`, `cancelLabel`, `hoursLabel`,
`minutesLabel`, and `meridiemLabel` change the panel's words, for example to translate them. The AM
and PM options themselves are always in English; that's a known gap.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="States">

An empty field shows its `placeholder`. `invalid` gives the field Text Field's error border and sets
`aria-invalid`; in a Field with `state="error"`, Field sets it for you. `disabled` disables the
field, so the panel can't open.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.States} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Controlled">

Pass `value` and `onChange` to own the time, and `open` and `onOpenChange` to own whether the panel is
open. Without them, Time Picker keeps its own state, starting from `defaultValue` and `defaultOpen`.

<Example of={TimePickerStories.Controlled} />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Width">

The field is 240px wide by default and shrinks to fit a narrower container. Set a different width with
`className`. In a Table's editable cell, it fills the cell and takes the cell's size and the inline
context, and the panel lines its options up at the start with a line beside the chosen one.

</Subsection>

</Section>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- The field is a native `input` in Text Field's frame, read-only, with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`.
  Popup sets `aria-expanded` on it, and `aria-controls` pointing at the panel while it's open.
- Name it with Field, which wires its label to the input, or with `aria-label` or
  `aria-labelledby`. These go to the input, not the wrapper.
- In a Field, the message is added to the input's description, an error sets `aria-invalid`, and
  `required` sets the input's native `required`. Browsers don't check `required` on a read-only
  input, so check for an empty value yourself.
- The panel is a `dialog`, added at the end of the page. It has no accessible name yet; that's a
  known bug.
- Each column is a `listbox` named by `hoursLabel`, `minutesLabel`, or `meridiemLabel`. Its options
  are native buttons with `role="option"`, and the chosen one has `aria-selected="true"`. Only the
  chosen option in each column is in the tab order, and moving with the arrow keys chooses as it
  goes. Focus shows the shared Focus Ring.
- Opening moves focus to the chosen hour. Confirm, Cancel, and Escape return focus to the field.
  Tabbing away from the open panel leaves it open until Escape or a click outside; that's a known
  bug.
- The clock icon is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
- `disabled` is the native attribute: the input leaves the tab order.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves focus to the field. A disabled field is skipped. In the panel, moves from hours to minutes, AM or PM, Cancel, and Confirm.</Key>
  <Key keys="Enter">On the field, opens the panel. On an option, chooses it; on a button, presses it.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">On the field, opens the panel. On an option, chooses it; on a button, presses it.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Down">On the field, opens the panel. In a column, chooses the next option.</Key>
  <Key keys="Arrow Up">On the field, opens the panel. In a column, chooses the previous option.</Key>
  <Key keys="Home">In a column, chooses the first option.</Key>
  <Key keys="End">In a column, chooses the last option.</Key>
  <Key keys="Escape">Throws away the choices, closes the panel, and returns focus to the field.</Key>
</Keyboard>
