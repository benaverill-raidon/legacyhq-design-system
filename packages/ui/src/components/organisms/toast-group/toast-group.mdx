<Section title="Behavior">

<Subsection title="Setup">

Mount one Toast Group, at the end of the app's root so it comes last in the tab order, and call
`toast()` from anywhere. Toast Group stays where it's mounted in the page; it's only drawn in the
corner. Every Toast Group shows every toast, so a second one shows each toast twice.

<CodeSample
  code={setup}
/>

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The toast() API">

Each call adds a toast at the front of the stack and returns its id. The helpers set `tone`. A
`toast.loading` toast has no dismiss button and stays until it's updated or dismissed.
`toast.dismiss(id)` removes one toast at once, without the fade, and `toast.dismiss()` removes them
all. `toast.info`, the earlier name for `toast.information`, is still accepted.

<CodeSample
  code={toastApi}
/>

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Updating a toast">

Call `toast()` again with the same `id` to replace that toast in place; it keeps its spot in the
stack, and its timer starts over. Options you leave out keep their earlier values. The exceptions
are a loading toast's duration and dismiss button: when it's replaced with another tone, the result
gets the group's `duration` and a dismiss button unless you pass `duration` or `isDismissible`, so it
goes away on its own and can be closed.

<CodeSample
  code={updateLoading}
/>

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Stacking">

The newest toast is at the front. Collapsed, up to `maxVisible` toasts show, three by default: each
older one peeks 16px above the one in front and is drawn 5% smaller, and every toast shows its title
alone. Hovering the stack, or moving focus into it, spreads the visible toasts into a list with 8px
between them and shows their descriptions and actions. It stays spread out while either is true:
moving the pointer away doesn't collapse it while focus is inside. Toasts past `maxVisible` wait,
invisible and out of reach, and move into view as the ones in front go. To see it, press Save
changes at the top of this page a few times, then hover the stack or Tab into it.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Timing and dismissing">

Each toast is removed after it's been in view for its `duration`, or the group's: 5 seconds by
default. The timers pause while the stack is expanded and carry on with the time that was left when
it collapses. A waiting toast's timer doesn't start until it comes into view. A toast also goes when
its dismiss button is clicked, or when it's dragged sideways more than 80px; a shorter drag springs
back. It fades out, then it's removed. A loading toast has no dismiss button unless you pass
`isDismissible: true`: it's replaced with its result, which has one.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Motion">

Toasts move with the `move-long` recipe and fade in and out with `fade-long`, both 300ms. While a
toast is being dragged it follows the pointer with no transition. Under reduced motion every
transition is instant.

</Subsection>

</Section>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- Toast Group renders an `ol`, named by `label`, with each toast in an `li`, and each Toast's status
  is read before its title.
- A `role="status"` live region stays on the page, visually hidden, even with no toasts. Each toast's
  status, title, and description are added to it when the toast comes into view, and again when the
  toast is updated, so screen readers announce it politely. The cards in the list aren't live regions
  themselves (`role="none"`), so a toast isn't announced twice.
- Moving focus into the stack expands it and pauses the timers, so keyboard users can reach each
  toast's dismiss button and actions before they go. It stays expanded while focus is inside, even if
  the pointer leaves; moving focus out collapses it again.
- When a toast is dismissed from the keyboard, its button goes with it, so focus moves to the next
  toast in the list (the one behind it), else the previous one, skipping toasts with nothing to
  focus. When no toast is left to take it, focus goes back to where it was before it entered the
  stack, and only if that's gone, to the page. A pointer click leaves focus where it is, so the stack
  can collapse when the pointer leaves.
- The list sits where Toast Group is mounted in the page, so that's where it comes in the tab order.
  There's no key to jump to it, and Escape doesn't dismiss a toast.
- Waiting toasts past `maxVisible` are `inert` and `aria-hidden`: screen readers don't read them and
  Tab skips them until they come into view.
- Swiping is for pointers only; the dismiss button does the same from the keyboard.
- Under reduced motion every transition is instant.

<Keyboard>
  <Key keys="Tab">Moves into the stack, which expands it and pauses the timers, then through each toast's dismiss button and actions.</Key>
  <Key keys="Shift + Tab">Moves back; leaving the stack collapses it.</Key>
  <Key keys="Enter">On a dismiss button, fades the toast out and removes it, and moves focus to the next toast, the previous one, or back where it came from.</Key>
  <Key keys="Space">On a dismiss button, does the same as Enter.</Key>
</Keyboard>

</Section>
