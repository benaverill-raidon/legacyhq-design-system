<Section title="The problem">

Most of LegacyHQ is a list of something: the firm's matters, a matter's documents, a client's
filings. People come to a list to find one record, to compare several, or to change many at once.
Without one approach, one list sorts from its headers and the next from a menu, filters look
different on every screen, and people can't tell what they're acting on.

Three questions settle almost every case:

- **How do people read the records?** Compared across the same fields, read one at a time, or one
  record in full. The answer picks Table, Card, or Key Value List.
- **How do they find the ones they want?** Searching, sorting, filtering, and paging each have one
  control, and Table has a place for all of them.
- **What do they do with them?** Open one, act on one, act on several, or change one field where it's
  shown.

<Section title="Rules">

- **Choose by how people read the records, not by how many there are.** Records compared across
  the same fields go in a Table, even a short list. Records read one at a time, each with its own
  layout, go in Cards.
- **Open a record from a real link.** Put a strong Link in the column that identifies the row, or in
  the card's heading. Rows and cards aren't click targets: a Table row is selected only through its
  Checkbox, and Card handles no events.
- **Give each table a stable row id.** Pass a `getRowId` that returns the record's id. Selection is
  kept by id across pages and sorts, and the default id, the row's position in `data`, moves when
  the data is filtered.
- **Filter the data yourself, and go back to page 1.** Table shows the search and the filters but
  doesn't narrow `data`. When the search or a filter changes, set the page back to 1, so people
  start at the top of the new results.
- **Show what's applied.** Every filter that narrows the list shows as a filter Chip, in Table's
  `activeFilters` or a Chip Group above the list, and can be changed or removed there. Use Chips for
  filters, not Tags: Tags are references to records.
- **Say who a bulk action affects.** The selection bar counts every selected row, including rows on
  other pages or hidden by a filter, and a bulk action applies to all of them. Name the count in its
  confirmation, as "Delete 3 matters?" does.
- **Confirm what can't be undone.** Deleting, from a row or in bulk, asks in a Modal Dialog with
  `tone="error"`, then confirms with a toast, as <DocLink to="Patterns/Messages and Feedback" /> sets out.
- **Say why a list is empty.** When a search or filter finds nothing, pass Table an `empty` Empty
  State that says so and offers a way back, such as clearing the filters. The default, "No items to
  show", doesn't say that the search found nothing. <DocLink to="Patterns/Loading and Empty States" />
  covers lists with nothing in them yet.
- **Status is a Label.** Show a record's status in words with Label, whose tones mean something.
  Badge is for counts of one to four characters.

</Section>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- Table is a native `table`, named by a visually hidden `caption`: `caption`, or `title` when
  there's no caption. Give every table one. A sortable header sets `aria-sort` to `ascending`,
  `descending`, or `none`.
- Name each row's selection Checkbox for its record with `getSelectionLabel`, such as "Select Rivera
  v. Atlantic Insurance". Without it, every row's Checkbox is "Select row". When the selection
  changes, a polite live region announces the count, such as "3 selected".
- Name each row's action trigger and each editable cell's control for its row, such as "Actions for
  Rivera v. Atlantic Insurance" or "Status, Petition for probate", so they can be told apart out of
  context.
- Name a Chip Group of filters, such as "Active filters": only a named group has `role="group"`. A
  filter chip's value button is named by the field and its value together, such as "Status Open",
  and its remove button "Remove Status".
- Table's footer is a `navigation` region named for the table, such as "Matters pagination". A
  Pagination on its own is a `nav` named "Pagination" by default, so give each on a page its own
  name, as "Documents pagination" does.
- Card is a plain `div` that screen readers don't announce. Give each card's content its own
  semantics: an `article` named by its heading, in a list.
- Key Value List is a `dl`, with no name of its own. Put a heading before it. An inline control in a
  pair needs an `aria-label`, usually the key: the key doesn't name it.
- Say what an Avatar Group's people are in the text around it, such as a "Team" key.

</Section>
