<Section title="Purpose">

LegacyHQ links records as accounts, people, matters, roadmaps, and documents, and the system already
has a place for this work: icons for document generation, document templates, and signatures, a
Progress Bar whose own examples generate a document package, and a Split Button that offers "Create
from template". This template puts those pieces together as one workflow, for one matter, in a guided
flow with named steps, which is what Progress Tracker is for.

**This is a draft.** It was built before the designs for document generation were finished, so its
content is placeholder: the steps, the documents in the package, the details they need, the statuses,
and who signs. It shows how the system's components and patterns would carry the workflow, not how
LegacyHQ works. Everything it assumes is listed under Open questions, to be settled against the
designs.

</Section>

<Subsection title="Choices the components made">

- **Going back keeps everything.** The chosen documents and the details live above the steps, not in
  them. Changing either and generating again makes the whole package again.
- **Only the details the documents use.** Unchecking the will removes the executor from the details
  step, and each field's helper text names the documents that use it. A blank field from the matter
  says "Not in the matter yet."
- **Generate is a step, but not a link back.** Progress Tracker links only the steps before the
  current one, and only when they have an `href`. Generate has none, since returning to it means
  confirming the details again. The tracker has no links at all while the documents are generating,
  or once they've been sent.
- **The result arrives as the next step.** When the last document is generated, the flow moves to
  Review, and a toast says how many were made, as background work that finished does in
  <DocLink to="Patterns/Messages and Feedback" />.

</Subsection>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- **Headings.** The page heading, then one heading per step, then the table's title. In the product
  they're an `h1`, an `h2`, and an `h3`. The examples here start at `h3`, so they sit inside this
  page's outline. Form's title isn't a heading, so each step's heading is the screen's own.
- **Landmarks.** Breadcrumbs is a `nav` named "Breadcrumb", and Progress Tracker a `nav` named for the
  flow, "Document generation". Each step is a region named by its heading. The Review table is a
  native `table`, named by its title.
- **Where the step is.** The tracker's steps are an ordered list, so screen readers hear how many
  there are, and the current one is marked `aria-current="step"`. Steps already done are links, in
  the tab order; the others are text.
- **Focus between steps.** Continue, Back, a tracker link, and the end of generation each move focus
  to the new step's heading, which takes `tabIndex={-1}`, so screen readers start at the top of the
  step and keyboard users don't land at the bottom of the page. Focus moves only after something
  people did, never on first load.
- **Focus after a failed submit.** It moves to the summary, which is a polite live region but may not
  be read out when it appears with its content already in it. Each line links to its field and moves
  focus there. Checkbox Group's `required` adds hidden text to its legend but makes no checkbox
  required, so the step checks for at least one document itself.
- **Focus after a failure.** When generation stops partway, focus moves to the error, whose action
  tries again. Trying again removes the message, so focus goes back to the step's heading.
- **Progress.** The Progress Bar is named for the work, and its value is read as "3 of 5 documents
  generated" through `getValueText`. It isn't a live region, so each document isn't announced as it's
  made; the result is, by the toast and by focus moving to Review. The same count is shown beside the
  bar.
- **The confirmation.** Modal Dialog moves focus inside when it opens and returns it when it closes.
  After sending, the Send button is gone, so focus moves on to the step's heading, and the toast
  confirms through Toast Group's polite live region.
- **Row actions.** Each document's action trigger is named for it, such as "Actions for Last will and
  testament".

</Section>

<Section title="Open questions">

What the designs have to settle. Each is a placeholder in the template.

- **Where the flow starts and ends.** From the matter, perhaps from a Split Button's "Create from
  template"? Does it end back on the matter, or on its documents?
- **The steps.** Four steps, named Documents, Details, Generate, and Review. Is Generate a step of its
  own, or does it happen on Review? Is there a Cancel, and does leaving midway keep a draft?
- **Which documents.** The six templates, the five in the estate plan package, and their names, are
  invented. Where does the list come from: the matter's type, the firm's library, the state? A long
  list needs search or a Select instead of a Checkbox Group (see <DocLink to="Patterns/Collections" />).
  Are templates grouped, versioned, or chosen per client in a joint plan?
- **Which details, and where they're saved.** The seven details, which document uses which, and that
  all are required. Do edits here save back to the matter, or apply only to these documents? Can a
  document be generated with a blank to fill in later?
- **What a matter needs before generating.** Only a client, here. What else blocks it, and where do
  people fix it?
- **How generation runs.** One document at a time, counted on a bar. Can people leave the page while
  it runs, or cancel it? Does going back and generating again redo every document, or only the ones
  whose details changed?
- **When it fails partway.** Are the generated documents kept, and does trying again redo only the
  rest? Can people go on to Review with the documents that did generate?
- **Statuses.** Ready, Needs review, and Awaiting signature, and their tones. What else can a generated
  document be: a draft, edited, signed?
- **Needs review.** Who flags a document (the template, the generator, an attorney), what for, and
  does it stop the package from being sent?
- **Row actions.** Download and Regenerate. Is there a preview, editing in place or in Word, or
  removing a document from the package? What file format are the documents?
- **Sending for signature.** To the client only, or to witnesses and a notary too? Through which
  e-signature service? Does it ask first, and what happens after: a task, a roadmap step, the
  documents filed on the matter?

</Section>
