<Section title="Purpose">

A roadmap is one of the records LegacyHQ links to, beside accounts, people, matters, and documents.
It's made of tasks that are not started, in progress, or complete, and it has a completion: how many
of its tasks are done. The screen answers three questions for whoever opens it: how far along is
this, what's left and who has it, and what changed.

This template is a **draft**. It was built before the roadmap designs were finished, to see how far
the system's components go on their own. The client, the matter, the stage names, the tasks, and who
does them are placeholders for an estate plan, not how LegacyHQ works. Every assumption the screen
makes is listed under Open questions, for the designs to settle.

</Section>

<Subsection title="Table, not Cards">

Each task has the same fields, read across: its status, who has it, and when it's due. Table is for
records compared side by side across the same fields, and for editing a few fields of many records
in place. Card is for summaries that each have a layout of their own, and has no editing, sorting,
or selection. Patterns / Collections says to choose by how people read the records, not by how many
there are, so even a stage of two tasks is a table.

Table has no row groups, so each stage is its own Table, titled with the stage's name and how many
of its tasks are complete. They share one set of columns at fixed widths, with resizing off, so the
columns line up from stage to stage. A stage with no task that matches the filters is left out.

Card holds one thing: the completion summary, as Progress Bar suggests for its circular ring ("a
compact completion summary on a card or a tile"). Progress Tracker isn't used for the stages: it's
for a guided flow, and it shows position in a sequence, not completion.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Where the components stop">

- **The status is an icon beside a Label.** Label has no icon slot, so the task icon sits before it
  in the cell. The icon is decorative; the Label says the status in words.
- **The assignee cell shows the name, not the Avatar.** A single Select shows the chosen option's
  label only; its `icon` appears in the menu rows (and on multi-select chips). The Avatars show in
  the menu, in the filter chip, and beside the owner's name.
- **The filters sit above every stage, not in a Table.** Table's `activeFilters` belongs to one
  table, and these filters narrow all of them, so they're a Chip Group of their own, with the Add
  filter menu and a Clear filters button beside it.

</Subsection>

<Section title="Accessibility">

- **Headings.** In the product, the roadmap's name is the page's `h1` and each stage a level below.
  Here they start at `h3`, under this page's sections: the name is an `h3`, and each stage's Table
  title, and either Empty State, an `h4`.
- **Landmarks.** Breadcrumbs is a `nav` named "Breadcrumb". Each stage's table sits in a scroll
  region named by its stage, which takes focus so it can be scrolled from the keyboard; that's one
  tab stop per stage. The screen belongs inside the app's `main`, which the template doesn't draw.
- **Names.** Every control in a row is named for its task: "Actions for Draft the health care
  directives", "Assignee, Draft the health care directives", "Due date, Draft the health care
  directives". The filters are a group named "Active filters"; each chip's value button is named by
  its field and value, such as "Status Not started", and its remove button "Remove Status".
- **Status in words.** The task icons, the Avatars, the filter chips' previews, and the menu rows'
  icons are all decorative. The Label carries the status, and the visible name beside each Avatar
  carries the person.
- **Completion.** The ring is a progress bar named "Roadmap completion", read as "9 of 14 tasks
  complete" rather than a percentage. It isn't a live region, so a change isn't read out by the ring:
  the toast that confirms a status change says the new count.
- **Focus after a status change.** Choosing a status closes the menu and returns focus to the task's
  actions button. When the task no longer matches the filters, it leaves the list, so focus moves to
  the results line instead, which says how many tasks match. Removing a filter chip and clearing the
  filters from the Empty State move focus there too.
- **Focus after an edit.** Picking a due date closes the calendar and returns focus to its field.
  Choosing an assignee leaves focus in its field, unless the task leaves the filtered list, when
  focus moves to the results line.
- **Announced.** Toasts are read through Toast Group's polite live region, with their tone first:
  "Success: Task complete", then the task and the new count. The results line is a polite live region
  (`role="status"`), so a filter that changes the count is read out, including "0 of 14 tasks match"
  when the Empty State replaces the stages, since Empty State isn't a live region itself.
- **Loading.** One skeleton is labelled "Loading the roadmap", a polite status; the others are
  hidden. The loading table sets `aria-busy`.

</Section>

<Section title="Open questions">

Everything below is an assumption the draft makes, for the designs to settle.

- **Where a roadmap lives.** Under its matter, as the breadcrumbs show (Matters, the matter, the
  roadmap)? Or under the client, or both?
- **The key facts.** Client, matter, owner, and target date are guesses. Which does a roadmap have,
  and can they change here?
- **Stages.** Does a roadmap have stages at all? What are they called, are they in a fixed order, and
  does one have to finish before the next starts? Do finished stages collapse?
- **Task fields.** Name, status, assignee, and due date are guesses. Do tasks carry more: a
  description, documents, events, a priority? Does a task open a page of its own? Here its name is
  plain text, not a link.
- **Statuses.** The three task icons suggest not started, in progress, and complete. Are there others,
  such as blocked or skipped?
- **Assignees.** Can a task be assigned to the client, as some placeholder tasks are? To a team? To
  nobody?
- **Completion.** Is it the share of tasks complete, as here, or weighted by stage or task?
  At 0 Progress Bar now shows the ring's empty track, with no arc, beside the count: is that the
  right empty ring for a roadmap with nothing complete?
- **Late tasks.** No placeholder task is overdue. How does a task past its due date show, and does a
  complete task show when it was completed instead of when it was due?
- **Adding and arranging tasks.** How tasks are added, removed, and reordered, and whether a
  roadmap starts from a template. The Empty State's “Add a task” isn't wired.
- **Saving in place.** Do an assignee and a due date save as soon as they change, with no
  confirmation, as here? What happens when a save fails?
- **Undo.** The toast has no Undo; setting the status back from the task's actions does it. Should
  the toast offer Undo?
- **Filters.** Are status and assignee the filters people need? Should the filters be remembered, and
  should a stage with no matching task disappear, as here, or stay with its own empty row?
- **Who's told.** Does completing a task, or the roadmap, notify the client or the assignee?

</Section>
